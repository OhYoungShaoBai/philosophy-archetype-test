# Result Illustration Direction

The result illustrations use philosophical allegory characters, not literal philosopher portraits.

Core direction:
- Chinese-language product audience; characters should feel natural for Chinese users.
- Faces stay soft, turned away, rim-lit, or partially obscured. Avoid portrait-like identity.
- The set may include men, women, and androgynous figures. Do not make every archetype male.
- Avoid gender stereotypes: reason, principle, care, freedom, and community should not map to default gender roles.
- Keep a warm academy atmosphere with books, windows, paper, light, architecture, fog, fire, maps, and civic space.
- Avoid readable text, real philosopher likenesses, all-male scholar casts, fantasy armor, dark fantasy mood, and purple gradients.

Current character distribution:
- 理性的筑城者: feminine or androgynous East Asian protagonist.
- 雾中的怀疑者: masculine-presenting East Asian protagonist, face obscured by mist and lantern light.
- 意义的守夜人: feminine-presenting East Asian protagonist, solemn and intellectually strong.
- 荒诞的漫游者: androgynous East Asian protagonist at an open threshold.
- 准则的执灯者: feminine-presenting East Asian protagonist, principled without authoritarian styling.
- 尘世的修补者: masculine-presenting East Asian protagonist, gentle and practical.
- 自由的造火者: feminine or androgynous-feminine East Asian protagonist, creative and unsensationalized.
- 城邦的编织者: small mixed-gender East Asian group, no single leader dominates.
